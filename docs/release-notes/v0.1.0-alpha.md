**Free, offline background removal for your images.** This is the first public alpha of KreoKit Cutout: drop in a photo (or a whole folder of them), remove the background, fix the edges, put something new behind it and export. Everything runs on your own computer. No account, no upload, no watermark.

> ⚠️ **Alpha.** Expect rough edges. Please report problems at https://github.com/kreokit/cutout/issues (or ⋯ → Report a problem in the app).

## What it does

- **Remove backgrounds offline** with two AI models: **Fast** (about 1 second per image) and **Best** (sharper hair and fine detail, 10–30 seconds per image). Each downloads once, then works offline.
- **Fix the edges:** feather, shrink or grow the cutout, remove the dark fringe left by the old background, and erase or restore with a brush. Undo and redo everything.
- **New backgrounds:** transparent, a solid colour, a blur of the original photo (without a halo around the subject), or an image of your own. One background for all open images.
- **Export** PNG (with transparency), WebP or JPG, at the original size or for social posts (1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1080×1920, 1280×720), with **Fit** or **Fill** framing that never cuts off the subject.
- **Batch:** open many images, see each one's progress in a grid, pick Fast or Best per image, and **Export all** into one folder. Nothing is overwritten without asking.

**Requirements:** macOS 12 Monterey or later, Windows 10 (version 1903 or later) or 11, or Linux with glibc 2.35+ (e.g. Ubuntu 22.04+). On Intel / AMD computers the processor needs SSE4.2 and POPCNT (almost every Intel CPU since 2009 and AMD CPU since 2011); the app tells you if yours doesn't.

**AI models:** the first cutout downloads the Fast model (176 MB). Best is 205 MB on computers with more than 8 GB of memory and 198 MB on 8 GB computers; if free memory is low when you use Best, the app switches to the smaller version or asks before running. These one-time downloads are the app's only internet use.

## Installing an unsigned app

The alpha isn't signed with paid developer certificates, so your system will warn you the first time.

**macOS**
1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **KreoKit Cutout** to Applications.
2. Open it. If macOS says it "can't be opened" or "can't verify the developer", open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** (on macOS 12: **System Preferences → Security & Privacy → General**), and click **Open Anyway**, then confirm.
3. If macOS instead says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", that is the same missing signature, not real damage. Run this once in Terminal, then open the app again:
   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/KreoKit Cutout.app"
   ```

**Windows**
1. Run the `…-setup.exe` installer.
2. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Linux**
- AppImage: `chmod +x KreoKit.Cutout_*.AppImage`, then run it.
- Debian / Ubuntu: `sudo apt install ./KreoKit.Cutout_*_amd64.deb`.

## Known limitations

- Everything runs on the CPU (no GPU acceleration yet), so Best takes a while on older computers.
- **Fast** can miss dark hair on dark backgrounds or leave a thin dark rim along hair; use **Best** for portraits and pets (the app suggests it after a Fast cutout).
- Edits are kept while the app is open, not after you close it.
- Images only (PNG, JPG, WebP); no HEIC or video yet.
